Why Random?

We are excited to introduce a one-day remote working system. To ensure our operations remain highly efficient and coverage is evenly spread, your remote day is randomized each week. This keeps things fair and prevents predictable absences from impacting workflow.

System Rules & Guidelines

  • Randomized Weekly: Your remote day shifts week to week.
  • Holiday Guarantee: In the event of a public holiday, you still get a work from home day that week.
  • Office is Open: The office remains strictly open on your remote day if you prefer to come in.
  • Professionalism: It is still a workday, expectations remain the same. No excuses.
  • Check-in/Out Required: Check-in upon starting. Check-out requires a voice note detailing your day's work. Missing it counts as absent.
